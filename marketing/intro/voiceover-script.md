# Part 1

## 01. Your ground's own booking page

Hi! This is GoArena: your ground's very own booking page. In the next few minutes, I'll show you why grounds are moving to it, how a booking works, and how easy it is to run.

## 02. Stop paying for your regulars

Booking apps are good at bringing new players. But they take a cut of every booking, often ten to fifteen percent plus GST, and pay you the following week. Most of your bookings are regular teams who already know your ground. With GoArena, they book on your own page. No commission, the money comes straight to you, and nobody can post a public bad review.

## 03. One link for your ground

Your ground gets its own link, like book dot goarena dot in, slash, your ground's name. Put it on a QR poster at the gate, or share it on WhatsApp. Customers open it on their phone. There's nothing to download. You pay a small flat fee each month, and never a commission on bookings.

## 04. Three people, three views

Three people use GoArena. Customers book and pay, without any login. You, the owner, set the prices and see every booking and payment. Your helper only sees who's coming and when, never the money. You and your helper log in with the Google account already on your phone, with a PIN as a backup.

## 05. A customer scans your QR poster

A customer scans the QR code on your poster at the gate, or taps the link you sent on WhatsApp. Your booking page opens right away, with nothing to install.

## 06. They see your ground

They see photos of your ground, your facilities like parking, floodlights and drinking water, your prices, and a Google Maps link to find you.

## 07. They pick a court and a time

They pick a date, then the full ground for eight a side, or half the ground for five a side, then a free time. Green is free. Grey is already booked. Weekend prices apply on their own.

## 08. Never a double booking

If someone books the full ground, both halves close for that hour. If someone books one half, another team can still take the other half, but nobody can book the full ground. GoArena checks this every time, even when two people tap at the same moment, so you never get a double booking.

## 09. They enter their name and number

They type their name and phone number. No account, no password. The slot is then held for them while they pay, thirty minutes by default, so nobody else can grab it. If they don't pay in time, it opens up again on its own.

# Part 2

## 10. Two ways to get paid

You choose how customers pay. With automatic payments, they tap Pay, approve it in Google Pay or PhonePe, and the booking confirms itself in seconds. This uses your own Razorpay account, which charges a small fee per payment. Or keep it simple: they pay your UPI ID by copying it or scanning a QR with the amount filled in, and send you a screenshot. Either way, the money goes straight into your bank account, never through GoArena.

## 11. Screenshots: you check, then approve

For screenshot payments, the request shows up on your Today screen. Check your bank app to see the money has come in, then tap Approve. If something looks wrong, tap Reject, and the customer is told. With automatic payments, there's nothing to check. They confirm by themselves.

## 12. The booking is confirmed

Once paid, the slot is locked on your calendar, and the customer's page shows Confirmed, which they can show at the ground. Teams who call you or walk in can be added to the same calendar in two taps, and you can close a slot for maintenance. Everything stays in one place.

## 13. Cancellations and refunds, handled

Plans change, and GoArena handles it. If a customer paid online and cancels early enough, twenty four hours before by default, they tap Cancel and their refund goes back automatically. If you have to cancel, because of rain or repairs, you choose a full refund, part, or none, and GoArena sends it. Screenshot payments go on your Refund due list, so nothing is forgotten.

## 14. Your helper knows who's coming

Your helper sees only the upcoming bookings: name, phone number, time, and court. If a team is running late, he calls them with one tap. He never sees any money.

## 15. You're in control

From Settings, you change everything yourself: weekday and weekend prices for the full ground and each half, photos, facilities, opening hours, how long a slot is held, your cancellation rules, your helpers, and automatic payments. Changes show on your booking page straight away.

## 16. Ready-to-print QR posters

GoArena makes your posters for you, using a photo of your own ground, your ground's name, and your QR code. Pick from three designs. Download an A4 or A3 poster for the gate, a standee or a big banner that any print shop can print, a status image for WhatsApp and Instagram, or just the plain QR if you'd like to design your own. For big prints, use a sharp, clear photo of your ground.

## 17. We're right behind you

And you're never on your own. If you forget your PIN, if a payment looks stuck, or you want help setting up, the GoArena team can sort it out remotely.

## 18. Getting started takes a day

Getting started is simple. Sign up with your Google account at book dot goarena dot in, slash, signup. Add your UPI ID, a few photos, your prices, your helper's number and your map location. We check your page and switch on bookings, usually within a day. Then print your poster, share your link, and keep every rupee. Talk to us about the small monthly fee. There's never a commission.
